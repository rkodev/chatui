- Status: Not started

- Parallel-safe: no

- Read first: [M6 plan](../M6-managed-hosting-implementation-plan.md) sections 3.4, 4, and 7; [M6 milestone](../../milestones/M6-managed-hosting.md) workstream 4 and "Risks and decisions to confirm"; all M6 privacy, logging, support, domain/TLS, and provider-terms docs

## Goal

Obtain and record a dated privacy/legal review of the tenant-facing
documentation, correct any unsupported claim, and block publication until the
named reviewer approves it.

## Preconditions

- `M6-04.1` and `M6-04.2` are `Complete`.
- `M6-03.3` and `M6-02.3` are `Complete`, so provider terms and domain/TLS
  behavior are documented.
- A named privacy/legal reviewer exists from `DEC-M6-11`.
- If no reviewer is available, mark this task `Blocked`. Do not self-approve
  legal/privacy copy.

## Deliverables

- `docs/operations/M6/evidence/privacy-review.md`:
  - Reviewer name, role, date, and approval status.
  - Documents reviewed with paths and content hashes or commit reference.
  - A checklist covering:
    - What roksky can and cannot see.
    - Provider-direct and storage-direct traffic.
    - Logging fields, exclusions, retention, and access.
    - Support roles, no impersonation, audit, and no-recovery claims.
    - Provider and storage responsibility.
    - Region and data-residency statement.
    - No analytics, no hidden network destinations, and no prompt proxy.
    - Passphrase loss and export path.
    - Subprocessors/third parties, or an explicit "none confirmed" note.
  - Claims verified against actual edge configuration, logs, support roles, and
    the browser network trace (the trace may be finalized in `M6-06.1`; record
    the dependency if not yet available).
  - Required corrections and the commit/change that applied them.
  - Final approval decision: `approved`, `approved with conditions`, or
    `rejected`.
  - Open conditions and their owners.
- Corrections applied to the M6 privacy, logging, support, provider-terms, and
  related docs. Do not change product behavior to satisfy copy.
- A clear publication gate: if status is not approved, the privacy docs must not
  be represented as final and M6 cannot close.

## Implementation steps

1. Confirm the reviewer and schedule the review.
2. Assemble the review packets and links.
3. Walk the reviewer through the edge flow, logs, support roles, and docs.
4. Record every required correction with an owner.
5. Apply corrections without inventing new capabilities.
6. Re-check the corrected docs against the actual configuration.
7. Record the final decision and conditions in `privacy-review.md`.
8. If the reviewer rejects a claim, mark M6 blocked and update the README status.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A named reviewer and dated review record exist.
- [ ] Every required privacy/logging/support/provider/region claim was reviewed.
- [ ] Corrections are applied and linked to the review record.
- [ ] No claim is approved without operator evidence or a recorded condition.
- [ ] The final decision is explicit.
- [ ] Unapproved conditions remain open blockers.
- [ ] No secret, real key, passphrase, prompt, chat content, or real log sample
      is included.
- [ ] The publish gate prevents unapproved copy from being treated as final.

## Verification

```bash
test -f docs/operations/M6/evidence/privacy-review.md
grep -n "Reviewer\|Date\|Status\|approved\|condition\|Correction" docs/operations/M6/evidence/privacy-review.md
```

Manual cross-check of the approved docs against the recorded edge/log/support
configuration. The final network trace is linked from `M6-06.1` if it lands later.

## Out of scope

- Providing legal advice.
- Implementing operator logging, support, or subprocessor controls.
- Changing privacy behavior or product features.
- Approving claims the operator has not confirmed.

## Handoff notes

Record the reviewer, decision, conditions, and links to the corrected docs and
later evidence here.
