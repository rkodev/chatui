## Goal

Document the hosted tenant's backup/restore behavior and the user's
responsibility for provider and storage terms. The docs must make the
client-side encryption, non-destructive restore, passphrase-loss, and
no-managed-storage facts unambiguous.

## Preconditions

- `M6-03.1` is `Complete` and the onboarding guide exists.
- M5 is complete. Do not change backup behavior in this task.
- Do not claim ChatUI can recover a passphrase, restore a backup, or read chat
  content.

## Deliverables

- `docs/hosting/backup-and-restore.md`:
  - Where backup lives: `/settings/backup`, client-side only.
  - Configure and enable backup, then "Back up now".
  - Restore behavior: merge by default; a replace restore requires the exact
    typed confirmation. Cite the UI behavior, not a new promise.
  - Delete remote backup requires typed confirmation and does not delete local
    data.
  - What is backed up: conversations, messages, attachment references/bytes, and
    manifest metadata, all encrypted before upload.
  - What is not backed up or visible to ChatUI: provider keys, storage
    credentials, passphrases, plaintext chats, and server-side copies.
  - Passphrase-loss warning: ChatUI support cannot recover it.
  - Link to M5 cross-client restore and storage-inspection evidence.
  - Link to the export path and the onboarding/CORS docs.
  - A short recovery checklist for a new device.
- `docs/hosting/provider-terms.md`:
  - The user is responsible for complying with the terms of the provider they
    select.
  - OpenRouter may forward requests to an upstream provider under that
    provider's terms and data policy; link the provider catalog/docs.
  - ChatUI does not resell, proxy, or meter provider access in M6.
  - The user is responsible for their own storage provider, bucket policy,
    credentials, and backup passphrase.
  - No managed storage or data-recovery service is offered in M6.
  - Support cannot recover keys, chats, storage credentials, or passphrases.
  - A short "what ChatUI is and is not" table.
