## Goal

Document what Android streaming does and does not guarantee, so users and reviewers are not overpromised.

## Preconditions

- M3-04.5 is complete.
- Do not add a foreground service or WorkManager job in this task.
- Do not claim guaranteed background streaming.

## Implementation steps

1. Read `ChatRunner`, `MainActivity`, `AppStartup`, the manifest, and spec sections 9.4 and 9.9.
2. Write the document with these sections:
   - streaming while foregrounded;
   - best-effort short grace period when backgrounded, subject to OS limits;
   - what happens on process death (partial content preserved, interrupted state, regenerate);
   - no foreground service in M3;
   - no WorkManager or background sync before backup exists;
   - user-facing expectations and known limitations.
3. State clearly that long background responses are not guaranteed and a foreground service is a future enhancement.
4. Link the document from `M3-verification.md`.
5. Audit that no WorkManager job or foreground service was added.
