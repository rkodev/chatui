## Completion record

- Added `observeArchived()` to the conversation DAO/repository and
  `observeArchivedConversations()` to the conversation service.
- Added pin/unpin and archive/unarchive actions with a dedicated `Pinned`
  group that sorts before date groups.
- Added an in-list `Archived`/`Chats` toggle for the archive view.
- Added a buffered 5-second delete with an Undo bar. The row is hidden
  immediately; storage is mutated on timeout, on the next confirmed delete, or
  when the ViewModel is cleared. Undo cancels the buffer and restores the row
  and last-active pointer.
- Added repository, service, grouping, ViewModel, and Compose tests for
  ordering, archive filtering, delete/undo, and persistence.
- Verification: `make android-lint` passed, `make android-test` passed,
  `make android-assemble` passed.

## Handoff notes

- Undo approach: buffered 5-second delete in `ConversationListViewModel`
  (`UNDO_WINDOW_MS`); the row is filtered from the observed list while pending,
  and `onUndoDelete()` restores it without touching storage.
- Archive route: in-list `Archived`/`Chats` toggle on the Conversations
  destination. No separate shell navigation route was added, keeping the change
  inside the owned conversation feature.
